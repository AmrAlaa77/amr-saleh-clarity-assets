AI

Practical AI Tools Simplified

From curiosity to confident application.

From curiosity to application →

The organizational challenge

Employees are surrounded by AI tools, yet many remain unsure what to use, how to prompt effectively, how to verify outputs and how to apply AI safely to real workplace tasks.

Target participants

Managers, office professionals, L&D, HR, sales, operations and support teams seeking practical AI skills without a technical background.

Key outcomes

  • Select appropriate AI tools for workplace tasks
  • Write clearer prompts that produce useful outputs
  • Improve writing, research, analysis and presentations
  • Automate repetitive tasks and accelerate routine work
  • Verify outputs and reduce hallucination risks

Main topics

  • AI fundamentals in simple language
  • Prompt design and iterative questioning
  • Writing, summarizing, research and communication
  • Presentations, data analysis and visual creation
  • Workflow mapping and automation
  • Verification, privacy and responsible use
  • Saudi and GCC workplace use cases

Recommended format

Two-day, face-to-face workshop. Available in Arabic or English, with optional post-program application sessions.

Safety

BBS Safety Leadership

From compliance to ownership.

From compliance to ownership →

The organizational challenge

Safety procedures may be well established, yet incidents can continue when unsafe behaviours, normalization of risk, weak intervention and limited personal ownership remain unaddressed.

Target participants

Operations managers, frontline supervisors, HSE professionals, team leaders and safety champions working in high-risk environments.

Key outcomes

  • Recognize behavioural and human contributors to incidents
  • Conduct effective safety observations
  • Deliver constructive, non-threatening feedback
  • Intervene confidently when unsafe behaviour occurs
  • Strengthen speaking-up, accountability and safety ownership

Main topics

  • Foundations of Behavior-Based Safety
  • Human factors and risk perception
  • Safe and at-risk behaviours
  • The Antecedent–Behaviour–Consequence model
  • Safety observation and feedback
  • Psychological safety and speaking up
  • Leadership language, accountability and ownership

Recommended format

Two-day, face-to-face workshop. Available in Arabic or English, with optional post-program application sessions.

Data

Power BI

From data to better decisions.

From data to decisions →

The organizational challenge

Organizations collect large volumes of data, yet decision-makers often depend on scattered spreadsheets, manual reporting and delayed insights.

Target participants

Managers, analysts and professionals in sales, supply chain, finance, HR and operations with basic Excel knowledge.

Key outcomes

  • Import, clean and transform workplace data
  • Build clear and interactive dashboards
  • Create essential DAX measures and KPIs
  • Identify trends, gaps and business opportunities
  • Translate dashboard insights into management actions

Main topics

  • Power BI interface and analytical workflow
  • Data cleaning and transformation with Power Query
  • Data modelling and table relationships
  • DAX measures and calculated columns
  • Data visualization and dashboard design
  • KPIs, filters, drill-through and data storytelling
  • Saudi and GCC workplace use cases

Recommended format

Two-day, face-to-face workshop. Available in Arabic or English, with customized datasets and optional post-program application sessions.

Strengths

Gallup CliftonStrengths

From talents to team performance.

From talents to performance →

The organizational challenge

Many teams operate without a shared, positive language for talent. Managers often focus on fixing weaknesses instead of building on what employees do best, leaving hidden strengths underused and engagement low.

Target participants

Managers, team leads, HR and L&D professionals, and any team looking to build stronger self-awareness and collaboration.

Key outcomes

  • Identify personal top talent themes via the CliftonStrengths assessment
  • Translate talents into daily workplace behaviours
  • Build stronger, more balanced teams by leveraging complementary strengths
  • Improve engagement and motivation through strengths-based feedback
  • Create a shared strengths language across the team

Main topics

  • Introduction to the Gallup CliftonStrengths framework
  • The 34 talent themes and four domains
  • Individual strengths debrief and reflection
  • Applying strengths to roles, teams and leadership
  • Strengths-based feedback and coaching conversations
  • Building a strengths-based team culture

Recommended format

One-day, face-to-face workshop, including an individual CliftonStrengths assessment prior to the session. Available in Arabic or English.
