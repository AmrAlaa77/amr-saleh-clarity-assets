Flagship programs

AI+

AI by Doing

A practical workplace lab. Hands-on AI tools, real workflows, real impact.

  • Cognitive load theory (Sweller) — hands-on practice beats passive lecture for retention
  • System 1 / System 2 thinking (Kahneman) — knowing when to trust AI output and when to slow down and verify
  • Learning loop: try it on a real task → reflect → refine the prompt
  • Automation bias awareness — catching errors instead of rubber-stamping AI answers
Wellbeing+

Emotionally Unbreakable

Intelligence, resilience & wellbeing. Build mental strength, emotional agility and sustainable wellbeing.

  • Polyvagal theory (Porges) — regulating the nervous system under pressure
  • Growth mindset (Dweck) — reframing setbacks as data, not identity
  • Emotional granularity (Barrett) — naming emotions precisely to regulate them
  • Daily resilience routines that hold up under real workplace stress
Focus+

Focus Under Fire

Winning in a world of distractions. Cut through the noise, master your focus and achieve what matters.

  • Attention restoration theory — structured breaks that actually reset cognitive capacity
  • Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer) — "if-then" planning to protect deep work
  • Catching autopilot distraction with System 1 / System 2 awareness
  • Designing your digital environment to lower the cost of interruptions
Strengths+

Gallup CliftonStrengths

From talents to team performance. Uncover what each person does best and turn it into a strengths-based team culture.

  • CliftonStrengths assessment — identifying each person's top talent themes
  • Four domains framework — Strategic Thinking, Relationship Building, Influencing, Executing
  • Translating strengths into daily behaviours and team roles
  • Building a shared, positive language for talent across the team

Training programs

Soft Skills

Leadership for First-Time Managers
  • Situational Leadership (Hersey & Blanchard) — adapting your style to each team member's readiness
  • Tuckman's stages (forming–storming–norming–performing) for new teams
  • Self-efficacy (Bandura) — building a new manager's confidence early
  • A first-90-days plan: role clarity, expectations, quick wins
Strategic Thinking
  • Cynefin framework (Snowden) — matching your decision approach to the type of problem
  • Slowing down for high-stakes calls (System 1 vs. System 2)
  • SWOT and scenario planning as structured thinking tools
  • Moving from reacting to events to seeing patterns
Stakeholder Management
  • Power/Interest mapping — who needs what kind of engagement
  • Social exchange theory — trust as something built through reciprocity over time
  • Perspective-taking to understand what's really driving a stakeholder
  • A simple communication cadence that keeps people aligned
New Hires Essentials
  • Organizational socialization — how newcomers "learn the ropes" fastest
  • Sequencing onboarding information so it actually sticks
  • Psychological safety (Edmondson) in the critical first weeks
  • A clear 30-60-90 day plan
Coaching & Mentoring
  • The GROW model (Goal, Reality, Options, Will) for coaching conversations
  • Self-determination theory (Deci & Ryan) — autonomy, competence and relatedness as motivators
  • Active listening and powerful, open questions
  • Feedback that builds ownership instead of dependency
Feedback & Performance Management
  • The SBI model (Situation-Behavior-Impact) for precise, non-defensive feedback
  • Radical Candor (Scott) — caring personally while challenging directly
  • The SCARF model (Rock) — how feedback can trigger a threat response, and how to avoid it
  • Turning feedback into a forward-looking action, not a verdict
Professional Office Management
  • Getting Things Done (Allen) — capturing and organizing workload reliably
  • The Eisenhower Matrix for urgent vs. important
  • Attention residue (Leroy) — the hidden cost of task-switching
  • Simple systems for inbox, calendar and follow-through
Change Management
  • Kotter's 8-Step model for building and sustaining change
  • The ADKAR model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement)
  • Loss aversion (Kahneman & Tversky) — why people resist change more than they seek gain
  • Communication and involvement as the real resistance-reducers
Creative & Innovative Thinking
  • Design Thinking — empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test
  • Six Thinking Hats (de Bono) for structured parallel thinking
  • Separating divergent idea generation from convergent evaluation
  • Reframing constraints as creative fuel, not blockers

Sales

Selling Skills
  • Consultative / SPIN selling — Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff questions
  • Cialdini's principles of influence — reciprocity and social proof, used ethically
  • Building trust before you pitch: know, like, trust
  • Handling objections without triggering defensiveness
Negotiation Skills
  • BATNA — knowing your best alternative before you negotiate (Fisher & Ury)
  • Anchoring bias — why the first number on the table matters
  • Principled negotiation: separate the people from the problem, focus on interests
  • Reading the room and managing your own emotions under pressure

Safety Leadership in High-Risk Industries

  • Psychological safety for speaking up about hazards
  • Leadership language that builds ownership, not just compliance
Power BI
  • From raw data to decision-ready dashboards
  • Data storytelling: visual hierarchy and cognitive ease
  • Trustworthy numbers — clean, modelled, verified data
  • Workflow: import → model → visualize → decide
AI Tools for the Office
  • Prompt design as a learnable skill, not a trick
  • Verification habits that counter automation bias
  • Matching the right tool to the right task
  • Building repeatable, reusable AI-assisted workflows
